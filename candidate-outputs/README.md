# Candidate Outputs

> **Status:** active
>
> **Reviewed:** 2026-09-24

This area holds provisional figures and tables while their evidence, provenance,
and intended thesis use are being evaluated. Assets here are candidates, not
qualified or selected thesis results.

- `figures-source/`: editable sources, working exports, and source images for
  candidate figures.

When a candidate is qualified and selected for thesis use, move its required
source and export into `finalized-outputs/`, add a provenance record under
`research-context/result-provenance/`, and update the
[figure and table backlog](../research-context/figure-and-table-backlog.md).
