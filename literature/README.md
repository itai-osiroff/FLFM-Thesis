# Literature

> **Status:** active
>
> **Reviewed:** 2026-09-26

This area holds external sources and author-written notes that support the
thesis. Keep the source material distinct from the author's interpretation and
from claims that have already been selected for the thesis.

## Contents

- [Reading notes](notes/README.md): focused summaries and a reusable note
  template.
- [Thesis examples](thesis-examples/README.md): comparative examples and the
  documented role of Hadar's thesis.
- [Writing guidance](writing-guidance/README.md): preserved microscopy-writing
  report, critical assessment, source register, and links to the working digest.
- `external-resources-original-note.txt`: an earlier index of external
  resources.

Paper and book PDFs may be stored locally under this area, but Git ignores
literature PDFs by default. Keep citations and useful analysis in tracked notes
so the thesis work remains navigable without relying on the binaries.

For each important source, record its central claim, relevance to this thesis,
useful equations or figures, and the claims it may support. Update the
[planning claim tracker](../planning/claim-tracker.md) when source evidence
supports a specific thesis claim.
