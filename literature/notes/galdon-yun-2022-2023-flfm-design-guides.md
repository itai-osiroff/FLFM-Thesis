## Why This Matters

These guides are practical support for Chapter 3 and Chapter 4. They help turn
FLFM from a conceptual citation into an optical design and setup problem:
objective choice, relay lenses, aperture stop, microlens array, camera, double
conjugation, and numerical-aperture matching.

## Key Claims

- The 2022 guide condenses FLFM design choices into practical relationships
  among spatial resolution, field of view, depth of field, and host microscope
  hardware.
- The 2023 guide emphasizes setup requirements including double optical
  conjugation and NA matching.
- These sources are especially useful for setup and model caveats, not for
  proving the thesis' reconstruction performance.

## Safe Wording

The Applied Optics FLFM guides frame FLFM as a practical optical design and
alignment problem, with system parameters tied to spatial resolution, field of
view, depth of field, conjugation, and NA matching. This thesis can use them to
support setup constraints and caveats without presenting them as result
evidence.
