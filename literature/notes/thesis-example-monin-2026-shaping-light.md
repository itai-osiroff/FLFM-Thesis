# Thesis Example - Monin 2026 Shaping Light From Microscopy To Holographic Displays

> **Status:** reference
>
> **Reviewed:** 2026-09-24

## Citation

Sagi Monin, *Shaping Light from Microscopy to Holographic Displays*, PhD
thesis, Technion - Israel Institute of Technology, January 2026.

This note treats the PDF as a structure, tone, and presentation example. Do not
use it as technical evidence for the FLFM thesis unless a specific technical
connection is later justified.

## Why This Matters

This is a paper-style PhD thesis that ties several contributions together under
a broad "shaping light" theme. It is useful for seeing how to introduce a broad
field, separate contribution chapters, and use appendices for detailed optical
systems, calibration, and derivations.

For the current MSc thesis, the useful lesson is not to copy the breadth, but to
borrow the clean funnel from motivation to contribution.

## Useful Structural Patterns

- Front matter includes abstract, abbreviations/notations, math notation, and a
  publication list.
- Chapter 1 uses a broad-to-specific funnel: shaping light, deep tissue imaging,
  wavefront correction families, and explicit contribution subsections.
- Contribution chapters are semi-self-contained.
- Chapters bridge from previous limitations to the next technical question.
- Appendices hold optical-system details, calibration, derivations, and extra
  results.

## Tone And Wording Lessons

- Use explicit contribution statements after reviewing prior approaches.
- Use active research prose such as "we propose", "we show", and "we validate"
  when the evidence supports it.
- Explain the intuition before equations or optimization objectives.
- Keep conclusion claims tied to demonstrated results.

## Figure And Equation Lessons

- Start with schematic figures that establish geometry, notation, and system
  layout.
- Use multi-panel result figures that show target/reference, baseline, proposed
  output, and diagnostic visualization.
- Captions should explain both what is shown and why it matters.
- Long derivations should move to appendices once the main text has enough
  information to follow the method.

## Thesis Placement

| Current thesis area | Lesson |
| --- | --- |
| Chapter 1 | Use contribution subsections and a clear thesis map. |
| Chapter 2 | Keep literature review tied to the optical/computational bottleneck. |
| Chapter 5 | Introduce method intuition before equations and algorithm details. |
| Chapter 6 | Put system/calibration details in the main text only when needed for reproducibility; push exhaustive component lists to appendix. |
| Appendices | Use appendices for optical setup, calibration, extra derivations, and additional result panels. |

## What Not To Imitate

- Do not let a broad theme dilute a compact MSc thesis.
- Do not rely on contribution-chapter independence if the thesis needs one
  continuous proof-driven argument.
- Check title/chapter mappings and publication-list consistency carefully.
- Avoid long captions where the takeaway is buried.

## Possible Thesis Use

- Structure reference for contribution framing.
- Tone reference for explaining optical-computational methods.
- Appendix organization model for calibration, derivations, and additional
  results.
