## Evidence

The LED array supplies illumination-angle diversity. The authors extend geometric refocusing with coherent multislice reconstruction and add dark-field data to expand the accessible lateral and axial frequency bands.

- PDF p. 1, search for “multislice coherent model”.
- PDF p. 1, search for “dark-field images”.
