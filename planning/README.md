# Thesis Planning

> **Status:** active
>
> **Reviewed:** 2026-09-26

Use this area to organize the thesis argument, writing work, evidence decisions,
and supervisor follow-up.

## Working Documents

- [Chapter completion and parallel review plan](chapter-completion-review-plan.md):
  November 3 manuscript assessment, complete-chapter handoff gates, evidence
  priorities, and writing/review sequence.
- [Thesis outline](thesis-outline.md) and [revised structure](revised-thesis-structure.md):
  working chapter order and purpose.
- [Chapter synthesis map](chapter-synthesis-map.md): converts reading and
  research context into chapter work packages.
- [Thesis writing standard](writing-standard.md): argument, chapter pacing,
  placement of evidence, and thesis completeness.
- [Prose and figure standard](prose-and-figure-standard.md): sentences,
  paragraphs, visual comparisons, and captions.
- [Technical writing guide](technical-writing-guide.md) and
  [stage worksheet](templates/technical-stage.md): models, algorithms, notation,
  parameters, evaluation, and reproducibility.
- [ARS writing workflow](ars-writing-workflow.md): evidence-to-draft guidance
  and task-specific use of ARS-Codex.
- [Claim tracker](claim-tracker.md): connects thesis claims to sources and
  internal evidence.
- [Open questions](open-questions.md), [supervisor meetings](supervisor-meetings.md),
  and [writing log](writing-log.md): decisions and next steps over time.
- [Template and reference provenance](upstreams.md): records upstream thesis
  template and comparative-reference sources.

Start with the outline and structure, then use the synthesis map to identify
writing tasks. Keep claims provisional until the supporting evidence and
result provenance are clear.
