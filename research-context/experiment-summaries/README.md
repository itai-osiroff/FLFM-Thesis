# Experiment Summaries

> **Status:** active
>
> **Reviewed:** 2026-09-24

This folder currently contains curation indexes for selected result families.
They help identify scenes and explain why they may matter to the thesis; they
are not final experimental reports or complete result provenance.

- [Eyedrop result aggregation](eyedrop-result-aggregation.md): shortlist of
  human-selected eyedrop scenes and their qualitative relevance.
- [Chicken result aggregation](chicken-result-aggregation.md): curation
  shortlist for chicken-breast result candidates.

Before citing a candidate as a thesis result, confirm its exact dataset, run,
code revision, parameters, and supporting metrics. Record selected figure or
table origins under `../result-provenance/`.
