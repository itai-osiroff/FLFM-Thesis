> **Status:** active

This area holds author-created material that may support the thesis but is not
part of the active LaTeX project.

Keep only material that is useful to thesis writing or interpretation here.
Move selected final figures and tables to `finalized-outputs/`; keep material
used directly by the compiled thesis in `overleaf/`.
